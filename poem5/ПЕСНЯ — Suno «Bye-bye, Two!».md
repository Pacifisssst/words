# Song for Suno: "Bye-bye, Two!"

This is the reward song for poem 2. The verses are the poem word for word, and the chorus is short and easy to sing.

In Suno: open **Custom**, then paste the three fields below.

## Title

```
Bye-bye, Two!
```

## Style of Music

```
happy kids pop, bouncy piano and handclaps, xylophone, cheerful young female vocal, very clear English diction, slow-medium tempo 100 bpm, simple catchy singalong melody, fun, playful
```

## Lyrics

```
[Intro]
Hello, hello!

[Verse 1]
Hello, hello! I'm eleven,
English isn't hard, it's heaven!
I've got a ruler, I've got a pen,
I can count from one to ten.

[Chorus]
Red and yellow, green and blue,
Bye-bye, bye-bye, little two!
Red and yellow, green and blue,
Bye-bye, bye-bye, little two!

[Count]
One, two, three, four, five,
Six, seven, eight, nine, ten!

[Verse 2]
Hello, hello! I'm eleven,
English isn't hard, it's heaven!
I've got a ruler, I've got a pen,
I can count from one to ten.

[Final Chorus]
Red and yellow, green and blue,
Bye-bye, bye-bye, little two!
Red and yellow, green and blue,
Bye-bye, bye-bye, little two!

[Outro]
Bye-bye, two! Hello, five!
```

## Tips

- Numbers are written as words on purpose. With digits, Suno can skip them or sing them strangely.
- Choose the version where every word is clear. The song should help the girls remember the poem.
- If the voice is too fast, add `slow and clear` at the start of Style and generate again.
