# Song for Suno: "I Know What to Do"

This is the reward after the poem. The verses are the poem word for word, so the song helps the girls remember it. The chorus is new and short, so they can sing along on the first listen.

In Suno: open **Custom**, then paste the three fields below.

## Title

```
I Know What to Do
```

## Style of Music

```
upbeat kids pop, bright ukulele and handclaps, bouncy bass, cheerful young female vocal, very clear English diction, slow-medium tempo 100 bpm, simple catchy melody, school singalong, happy, fun
```

## Lyrics

```
[Intro]
(Clap, clap!) One, two, three, four!

[Verse 1]
I have got a pencil case,
I have got a happy face!
I have got a book that's blue,
And a pen, and homework too!

[Chorus]
Bye-bye, two! Hello, five!
I know what to do!
Bye-bye, two! Hello, five!
I can do it — you can too!

[Verse 2]
Yesterday I got a two,
Now I know what I must do:
Read and learn and say it right —
Get a five! Oh, what a sight!

[Chorus]
Bye-bye, two! Hello, five!
I know what to do!
Bye-bye, two! Hello, five!
I can do it — you can too!

[Bridge]
(Read it!) Read it!
(Learn it!) Learn it!
(Say it!) Say it right!

[Final Chorus]
Bye-bye, two! Hello, five!
I know what to do!
Bye-bye, two! Hello, five!
I can do it — you can too!

[Outro]
Get a five! Oh, what a sight!
```

## Tips

- "two" and "five" are written as words on purpose. With the digits 2 and 5, Suno sometimes sings "number two" or skips them.
- Choose the version where every word of the verses is clear. The verses are the poem the girls must say by heart.
- If the voice is too fast, add `slow and clear` at the start of Style and generate again.
